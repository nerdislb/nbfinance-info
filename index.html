What this project is

nbfinance is a private household prototype. The intended pilot is one manually initiated local view of booked balances for two own/co-held EUR current accounts. There are no payments, transactions, card feed, combined balance, financial-result archive, automatic polling or phone sync in this pilot.

The application, source code and financial data remain private. These pages explain its limited scope; they do not distribute the application or offer access to other people. An account balance is not a spending allowance, and unknown card obligations are not zero.

What this website is not

This is an information-only site. It does not offer account login, bank authorisation, payment initiation, application downloads or a callback endpoint. It contains no financial snapshots, bank credentials or finance-application source code.

Never enter bank details here or add tokens, authorisation codes or financial information to these URLs. Future bank authorisation must use a separately configured official provider and bank flow.

Draft notices

Privacy information separates website hosting, the intended local bank-data flow and contact email, including their different retention limits. Usage information explains private eligibility, balance limitations and the separate registration/linking/access approvals.

Agreement to provide information pages does not settle contact/hosting arrangements or adopt the proposed procedures. Both notices remain review drafts pending operator approval; neither grants bank access or promises provider acceptance.
